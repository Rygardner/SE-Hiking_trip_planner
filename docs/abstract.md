Planning a hike can be a great effort. It requires knowledge of your desired route, weather, planned date/time, appropriate equipment, nearby attractions, and expenses you can expect to pay while on the hike. This can be difficult to keep track of, especially if you love variety in your life. The solution to this problem is to bring all these factors together into an easy-to-read and accessible hiking planner. This is where My Trailside provides a solution. With My Trailside, you can easily track, plan, and revisit custom made hiking trips. My Trailside allows you to select specific trail(s) on the interactive map via a map search function and plan the entirety of your trip, from hotels to desired routes you wish to travel. My Trailside features trail selection, weather prediction, recommended wear, date selection, fuel costs, account creation to save your personalized trips via the trip log or favorites, and nearby hotels and pricing via google integration. My Trailside is the all-in-one hiking planner that takes the confusion out of planning, and allowing you to spend more time hiking.
